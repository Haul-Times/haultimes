DAT: Van spot rates hit $3.11 as truck availability climbs 9%

DAT Freight & Analytics reported that broker-to-carrier spot rates for van averaged $3.11 a mile for the week of Nov. 7-13, up 4 cents, with reefer at $3.70 and flatbed at $3.61. Total equipment posts rose 9% to 187,501, while load posts fell 6% from the prior week to 2.70 million. Dean Croke, an industry analyst at DAT, said van linehaul rates are 36% above last year. Croke said year-over-year gains should narrow after Thanksgiving, which falls on Nov. 26, as van and reefer rates typically build into the holiday weeks. DAT's 35-day forecast has van linehaul rising to $2.36 a mile by Dec. 17.
