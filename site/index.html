Truckstop, FTR: Refrigerated spot rates surge beyond seasonal expectations

Broker-posted spot rates in the Truckstop.com system rose for dry van, refrigerated and flatbed loads in the week ended Nov. 20, according to FTR. Refrigerated rates led the gains, jumping 18.1 cents and running 44.5% above the same 2025 week. Dry van rates rose 2.3 cents, and flatbed rates rose 2 cents, which was their first increase in five weeks. Total load activity climbed 2.3% week over week, and the total market broker-posted rate rose 2.6 cents, its first increase in seven weeks.
