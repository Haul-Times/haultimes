CBSA reminds travellers of upcoming closure of the Remote Area Border Crossing Program and expansion of telephone reporting

The Canada Border Services Agency said the Remote Area Border Crossing Program is set to shut at 11:59 p.m. on November 30, 2026. After that date, travellers entering Canada at remote locations will need to call in through designated telephone reporting sites. The CBSA said 17 new telephone reporting sites are already operational, and it has identified more locations after consultations with local and Indigenous communities and other interested parties. Details on the additional sites will be added to the CBSA directory as they open.
