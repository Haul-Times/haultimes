Trucking By the Numbers: Driver stats, for-hire vs private

13 days ago · Published Nov 11, 2026 · United States
Reported by FleetOwner Read the original article at FleetOwner ›
Share

FleetOwner's annual Trucking By the Numbers feature looks at driver figures for for-hire and private fleets. Citing the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, it puts the number of heavy- and tractor-trailer truck drivers at about 2.2 million in 2025. According to the American Transportation Research Institute, the ratio of drivers to trucks dropped to 0.9 that year, and for-hire annual turnover was about 44.2%, down from 48% in 2024. The article says turnover was highest at the largest truckload fleets, at 70.7%. A National Private Truck Council survey of 89 private fleet respondents reported a 17.1% turnover rate, with about 58% of private fleet drivers able to go home every night. Compared with the ATA annualized salary, the surveyed private fleets paid about 19% higher wages.

Summary of reporting by FleetOwner. HaulTimes did not report this story.
