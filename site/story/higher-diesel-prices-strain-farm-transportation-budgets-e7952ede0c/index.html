Higher diesel prices strain farm transportation budgets

8 days ago · Published Nov 17, 2026 · United States
Reported by Iowa Agribusiness Radio Network Read the original article at Iowa Agribusiness Radio Network ›
Share

Higher diesel prices are adding costs for Iowa farmers and trucking operators, according to Matt Wells, vice president of the Mid-West Truckers Association. Wells cited refinery shutdowns, declining fuel reserves and the war between Russia and Ukraine as factors tightening supply. He said two California refineries were shut down and a refinery in Joliet, Illinois, was offline for seven to ten days, while Ukrainian attacks damaged Russian refining facilities.

Wells said transportation rates and operating budgets will need to reflect the higher costs, potentially for the next year to year and a half, and he does not expect diesel prices to fall significantly soon. He also cautioned that fuel prices do not always return to earlier levels after a large increase. On red-dyed diesel, Wells said the modern dyed product is essentially the same fuel as clear highway diesel, and that its use on public roads remains subject to federal and state tax requirements and any specifically authorized exemptions.
