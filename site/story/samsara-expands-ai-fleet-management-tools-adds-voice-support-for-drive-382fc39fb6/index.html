Samsara expands AI fleet management tools, adds voice support for drivers

11 days ago · Published Nov 16, 2026 · United States
Reported by TheTrucker.com · Company announcement Read the original article at TheTrucker.com ›
Share

Fleet technology provider Samsara has opened its Agent Studio platform to all of its customers, letting trucking companies and other fleet operators build custom AI assistants. According to Samsara, use of its AI agents has grown sevenfold since June 2026, as customers apply them to fuel efficiency analysis, compliance monitoring and driver assistance. The agents can be set to respond to specific events or operating conditions rather than waiting for a user's question.

Samsara says the agents can flag possible hours-of-service violations before they occur by weighing scheduled stops, remaining driving time and shift hours. A voice feature can call a driver when the truck enters a geofence around a distribution center and give site-specific instructions. The company reports that voice interactions overtook chat within four weeks of the feature's launch, and that about 75% of organizations using voice agents have increased weekly usage.
