Descartes, Highway and Carrier411: The Technology Behind Freight Fraud Prevention

3 days ago · Published Nov 20, 2026 · United States
Reported by Logistics Viewpoints Read the original article at Logistics Viewpoints ›
Share

Logistics Viewpoints argues that freight fraud prevention depends on whether a carrier's verified identity holds from contract through physical pickup. It points to an October 2026 Transport Topics examination that described companies including Descartes and Highway connecting identity verification to tracking intelligence and carrier enrollment. The article also cites Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration warnings about broker and carrier identity theft, along with advice to check registration information and watch for suspicious contact changes. It recommends independent confirmation of high-risk changes, such as a new pickup contact, driver, equipment or payment instructions, made through a known channel rather than the incoming message. It also says a valid operating authority record does not by itself establish who is answering calls or receiving pickup instructions.
