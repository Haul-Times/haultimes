Commercial Driver's License: Nick Castello; Application for Exemption

4 days ago · Published Nov 24, 2026 · United States
Reported by Federal Register (FMCSA) Read the original article at Federal Register (FMCSA) ›
Share

The Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration is asking for public comment on an exemption application filed by Nick Castello. The request would let state licensing agencies issue non-domiciled commercial learner's permits and CDLs to drivers in humanitarian or protection-based categories, including Uniting for Ukraine, Temporary Protected Status, asylum and pending asylum. The notice does not signal how the agency will decide; FMCSA will grant or deny the request after reviewing the application, safety analyses and comments.

The notice points to a final rule published February 13, 2026, which bars these permits and licenses unless the applicant shows lawful immigration status. The listed categories under that rule are H-2A, H-2B and E-2. Castello, described as a U.S. Marine Corps veteran and disabled veteran business owner, asks for relief of up to five years or a shorter period, and says he collected 93 responses from drivers reporting recurring problems with non-domiciled transactions. Comments are due December 24, 2026, under docket FMCSA-2026-3268.
