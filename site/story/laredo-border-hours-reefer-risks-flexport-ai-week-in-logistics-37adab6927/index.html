Laredo Border Hours, Reefer Risks, Flexport AI: Week in Logistics

6 days ago · Published Nov 19, 2026 · Canada–US cross-border
Reported by Mexico Business News Read the original article at Mexico Business News ›
Share

U.S. Customs and Border Protection and Mexico's National Customs Agency, known as ANAM, extended a Saturday commercial truck processing pilot at Laredo's World Trade Bridge, with the window now running until 7 p.m. The roundup says the extension adds three hours and lifts Saturday crossings by 400 to 500 trucks, while easing bottlenecks on Sunday and Monday. It also notes that cross-border volumes have reached record highs.
