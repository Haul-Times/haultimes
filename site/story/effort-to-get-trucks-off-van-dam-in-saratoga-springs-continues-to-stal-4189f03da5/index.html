Effort to get trucks off Van Dam in Saratoga Springs continues to stall

3 days ago · Published Nov 10, 2026 · United States
Reported by WAMC Read the original article at WAMC ›
Share

Saratoga Springs residents and city officials are again pushing to move tractor-trailer traffic off Van Dam Street. The push followed a late-night crash between a Skidmore College bus and a tractor-trailer that blocked traffic for hours at Van Dam and Clinton streets, though no major injuries occurred.

A five-ton weight limit ordinance the City Council passed in December 2023 came down within days, because the state said city officials had no authority to pass local ordinances on the state-designated route. A city request for a traffic study outlined rerouting trucks via Route 9 from Northway exit 14 in Malta or via Route 50 through Ballston Spa. The state said de-designating a route requires documented operational deficiencies or a provable history of accidents. A neighborhood association study counted upward of 700 trucks a day, and the Trucking Association of New York warned that de-designation could push trucks onto other routes.
