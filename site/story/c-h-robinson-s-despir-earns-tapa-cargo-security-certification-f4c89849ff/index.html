C.H. Robinson's DeSpir earns TAPA cargo security certification

3 days ago · Published Nov 11, 2026 · United States
Reported by STAT Times Read the original article at STAT Times ›
Share

DeSpir Logistics, a C.H. Robinson company focused on high-value freight, has earned TAPA AMERICAS FBSR 2025 Tier 1 certification. The company describes DeSpir as among the first third-party logistics providers in North America to reach that top level for freight brokerage. TAPA, the Transported Asset Protection Association, sets cargo security and theft-prevention standards, and its Freight Broker Security Requirements certification is meant to reduce theft, fraud and loss on high-risk freight. According to the release, the certification covers DeSpir's shipment planning, monitoring, chain-of-custody controls and incident response. C.H. Robinson acquired DeSpir on June 22, 2026, and said the deal expanded its network of vetted, security-focused contract carriers. The article also cites Verisk CargoNet figures showing industrywide theft losses of nearly $725 million in 2025.
