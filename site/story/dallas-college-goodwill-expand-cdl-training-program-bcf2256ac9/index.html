Dallas College, Goodwill Expand CDL Training Program

8 days ago · Published Nov 20, 2026 · United States
Reported by Wisevoter Read the original article at Wisevoter ›
Share

Dallas College and Goodwill Dallas have teamed up with FleetForce Truck Driver Training to widen CDL instruction across Dallas-Fort Worth. The program covers Class A, B and C licenses and combines classroom work with hands-on training, with FleetForce instructors teaching at Goodwill facilities. The Dallas location is FleetForce's 17th in the U.S. The company's Next10,000 campaign aims to train 10,000 students through 2026 and cites about 6,100 local CDL openings in the region. Qualified graduates are guaranteed an interview with a large full-truckload carrier through FleetForce's Pre-Hire network. The article does not yet give enrollment figures or completion and placement rates for Dallas students.
