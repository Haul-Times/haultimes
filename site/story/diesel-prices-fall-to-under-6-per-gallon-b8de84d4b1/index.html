Diesel prices fall to under $6 per gallon

10 days ago · Published Nov 17, 2026 · United States
Reported by TheTrucker.com Read the original article at TheTrucker.com ›
Share

The national average diesel price fell 19 cents per gallon, from $6.127 to $5.937, according to the weekly report from the United States Energy Information Agency. Every reporting region dropped by double digits. The Central Atlantic had the smallest decline, from $6.412 to $6.309, while the Midwest had the largest, from $6.201 to $5.967. The article links the drop to slipping oil prices. On an early Tuesday, the Associated Press reported that Brent crude edged 49 cents lower to $92.93 a barrel and U.S. benchmark crude slid 51 cents to $86.23 per barrel.
