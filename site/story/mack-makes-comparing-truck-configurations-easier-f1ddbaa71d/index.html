Mack Makes Comparing Truck Configurations Easier

2 days ago · Published Nov 17, 2026 · United States
Reported by Heavy Duty Trucking Read the original article at Heavy Duty Trucking ›
Share

Mack Trucks has launched MyGarage, a free feature inside its online Truck Builder. Buyers can save, name, search and reopen multiple truck configurations, then send a saved build directly to a Mack dealer when they are ready to talk specifics. Users can also review truck images and selected specifications, and reopen a build to keep refining it. Separately, Truck Builder now lets customers add their company logo to a configured truck, a feature previously limited to Mack dealers.
