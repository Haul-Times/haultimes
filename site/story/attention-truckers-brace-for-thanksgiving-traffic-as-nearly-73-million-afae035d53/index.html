Attention truckers: Brace for Thanksgiving traffic as nearly 73 million travelers hit the road

Yesterday · Published Nov 24, 2026 · United States
Reported by NewsBreak: Local News & Alerts Read the original article at NewsBreak: Local News & Alerts ›
Share

AAA projects that nearly 73 million Americans will travel by car between Nov. 24-30, and the National Safety Council estimates 428 people could die in motor vehicle crashes during the Thanksgiving holiday period. That fatality estimate is 2% lower than last year's, but the council also projects 48,800 people could suffer injuries needing medical attention. INRIX expects the two days before Thanksgiving, Tuesday and Wednesday, plus Sunday and Monday afterward, to be busiest, with Thanksgiving Day itself lighter. The article advises truck drivers to add time to trip plans, expect sudden lane changes and sudden braking, leave extra following distance, and plan parking, fuel and meal stops around possible holiday closures. The council also estimates 104 more lives could be saved if all vehicle occupants wore seat belts.
