Trucking responsible for nearly $110 billion in cross-border freight for September

Yesterday · Published Nov 16, 2026 · Canada–US cross-border
Reported by TheTrucker.com Read the original article at TheTrucker.com ›
Share

Freight trucks moved $109.7 billion in goods across the U.S. border with Canada and Mexico in September 2026, a 27.6% gain on the prior year, according to Bureau of Transportation Statistics data released Nov. 13. All modes combined handled $161.1 billion, up 23.4%, and trucks made up 68% of that, more than six times rail's share. Mexico accounted for nearly 69% of truck value, at $75.4 billion, versus $34.3 billion for Canada. The data include imports and exports and are in current dollars, not adjusted for inflation, and they do not count truckloads or trucks crossing. The agency named Laredo and El Paso, Texas, and Otay Mesa, California, as leading truck crossings for Mexico freight, and Detroit and Port Huron, Michigan, and Buffalo, New York, for Canada.
