GTCC and Old Dominion Freight Line support future truckers

6 days ago · Published Nov 17, 2026 · United States
Reported by Yahoo Read the original article at Yahoo ›
Share

Guilford Technical Community College in Greensboro runs a truck driver training program that staff say has grown every year since it started six years ago. The certificate takes eight weeks of full-time study or 14 weeks of nights and weekends, and costs about $1,800 including books. Jan Knox, VP of the college advancement foundation, said graduates can often find work right away at a salary of $60,000 or more.

Old Dominion Freight Line, a trucking company headquartered in the Triad, is funding four $1,000 scholarships a year at the college, two of them for women. Chris Brooks, Senior VP of HR at the company, said the awards help people who might not otherwise afford a CDL or a diesel mechanic certification. The company said it has over 200 women drivers.
