Lawsuit targets trucking company in deadly Greenville Co. crash

13h ago · Published Nov 11, 2026 · United States
Reported by Yahoo Read the original article at Yahoo ›
Share

A wrongful death lawsuit has been filed against Contract Transportation Systems Co. (CTS) and one of its truck drivers over a fatal crash on White Horse Road in Greenville, South Carolina. The complaint was filed Nov. 4 in federal court, on behalf of the estate of Leonard Harding McPeak Jr. The suit alleges that the CTS driver, heading northbound, pulled into a median turn lane and backed the tractor-trailer across southbound lanes while trying to reach a Sherwin-Williams store, so McPeak's pickup struck the trailer on Jan. 7. The plaintiff says McPeak suffered severe injuries and died, and accuses the driver of negligence and of violating South Carolina traffic laws and federal motor carrier safety rules. The suit also accuses CTS of negligent hiring, retention, training and supervision, seeks actual, consequential and punitive damages, and has requested a jury trial.
