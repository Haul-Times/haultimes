Commercial Driver's License: Saia LTL Freight; Application for Exemption

7 days ago · Published Nov 18, 2026 · United States
Reported by Federal Register (FMCSA) Read the original article at Federal Register (FMCSA) ›
Share

The Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration is asking for public comment on an exemption application from Saia LTL Freight, a company headquartered in John Creek, Georgia. Saia wants commercial learner's permit holders in its driver training program to operate combination vehicles with double trailers while under the direct supervision of a CDL holder with a doubles/triples endorsement. The notice states that it does not indicate what decision FMCSA will ultimately reach.

Saia has requested a 5-year exemption from two rules, 49 CFR 383.93(b)(1) and 383.37(a). According to the filing, approximately 300 trainees a year would use a fleet of about 19 training vehicles. Comments are due December 18, 2026, under docket FMCSA-2026-2642. After reviewing the application, safety analyses and public comments, FMCSA will either grant or deny the request.
