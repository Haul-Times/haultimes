At least 14 charged in alleged $22.5 million California train cargo theft scheme

13h ago · Published Nov 19, 2026 · United States
Reported by Mezha.net Read the original article at Mezha.net ›
Share

Federal prosecutors said at least 14 people have been charged over an alleged scheme that stole cargo worth $22.5 million from freight trains in Southern California. Prosecutors say the crew hit at least 36 freight trains between February 2024 and August 2026, cutting air hoses to force them to stop. Prosecutors identified Nathan Daniel Angel, 21, of East Los Angeles, as the alleged leader, operating with his father and two uncles. Officials said a Los Angeles-based sneaker resale business, which was not an authorized Nike reseller, bought stolen sneakers and generated more than $1 million for the crew. The defendants are presumed innocent, and the maximum penalties include five years for conspiracy and 10 years for each theft count.
