Scottsbluff County Highway 71 Crash Kills Two Truck Drivers

5 days ago · Published Nov 19, 2026 · United States
Reported by Hoodline Read the original article at Hoodline ›
Share

Two semi drivers died after their trucks collided head-on on Highway 71, north of Lake Minatare Road, outside Scottsbluff, on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, at about 9:10 a.m., according to authorities. The northbound driver was identified as 66-year-old Wesley Barkell of Pine Bluffs, Wyoming, and the southbound driver as 38-year-old Robert Henderson of Hemingford, Nebraska. The crash happened in foggy conditions. Three other vehicles drove into the scene and were damaged by debris before emergency crews arrived, but their occupants were uninjured.

Highway 71 was shut down in the area for about nine hours. The Nebraska State Patrol is conducting a full crash reconstruction, including post-crash inspections of both commercial vehicles, and no cause for the collision has been released.
