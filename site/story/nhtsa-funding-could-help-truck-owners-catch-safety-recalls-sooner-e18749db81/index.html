NHTSA funding could help truck owners catch safety recalls sooner

4 days ago · Published Nov 9, 2026 · United States
Reported by TheTrucker.com Read the original article at TheTrucker.com ›
Share

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration said on Nov. 5 it will provide $1.5 million to help states improve how they notify vehicle owners about open safety recalls. Funded state programs would develop reliable notification methods tied to vehicle registration and renewal, measure how many recalled vehicles get repaired, and document results so other states can adopt successful practices. NHTSA also wants applicants to study how owners respond to different notification methods and track whether repairs follow. The program is not specifically aimed at commercial trucks, but the article notes that better alerts could help carriers and owner-operators plan repairs. States have until Feb. 4, 2027 to apply, and NHTSA has not said which states will receive grants or when new systems might start. The initiative does not set new commercial maintenance requirements.
