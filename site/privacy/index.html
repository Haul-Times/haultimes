Privacy

HaulTimes does not ask for your name or an account to read the site, and does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Visit counts. We count page views and where visitors came from (for example, Facebook) to see which pages are useful. No cookies are used and nothing is stored on your device. To count unique visitors, your internet address is turned into a scrambled code that changes every week and cannot be turned back into the address. The address itself is not stored.

Rate reports and market answers. When you report a rate or answer the weekly market question on the Freight Market page, we store only what you enter (lane, equipment, rate, distance, date, or your answer) and the same weekly scrambled code, used to stop spam and to make sure one person can't tilt the results. We never ask for names, company names or contact details there. We publish only combined figures, never single reports.

Location. For the weekly market question you choose your state or province yourself; it is remembered only on your device. Only the state or province is stored with an answer.

Messages sent through the contact form are used only to reply to you.

Questions: use our contact form. See also our Terms of Use.
